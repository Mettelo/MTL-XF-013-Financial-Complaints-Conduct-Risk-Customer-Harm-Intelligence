# Agreed Acceptance Criteria

1. **AC1:** Every rate metric documents its denominator; where a denominator is unavailable the metric is labelled as volume-only.
2. **AC2:** At least five headline figures reconcile directly to the FCA source workbook for selected firms/products.
3. **AC3:** No ranking uses raw complaint volume alone as a conduct-risk conclusion.
4. **AC4:** Outlier thresholds are documented and sensitivity-tested.
5. **AC5:** Firm/product identifiers and categories are standardised with an exception log.
6. **AC6:** Dashboard users can distinguish volume, rate, upheld and closure measures without ambiguous labels.
7. **AC7:** Every escalation recommendation cites at least two supporting indicators or a documented exception rationale.
8. **AC8:** A reviewer can reproduce the curated complaints table, benchmark outputs and validation checks from the approved FCA workbook.

## Sign-off Rule
Files existing is not sufficient. The review panel must verify these criteria from repository evidence.

Map each criterion to requirement(s), artefact(s), test/reconciliation evidence, owner and status.

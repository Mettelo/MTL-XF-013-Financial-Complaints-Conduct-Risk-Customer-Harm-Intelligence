# Business Context

## Simulated Client
**Confidential Regulated Financial Services Group**

## Business Pressure
Senior management receives complaint totals but cannot reliably identify which products, firms or complaint patterns may indicate deteriorating customer outcomes. Volumes, upheld rates, closure performance and contextual denominators are reviewed separately.

## Executive Decision
**Where should conduct and customer-outcome teams investigate first, and what evidence should trigger escalation, remediation or enhanced monitoring?**

## Stakeholders
- **Director of Conduct Risk:** accountable sponsor and decision owner.
- **Operational/Service Lead:** validates practical usability.
- **Data Owner/Steward:** challenges source authority, quality and definitions.
- **Finance/Risk/Performance stakeholder:** tests materiality and intervention logic.
- **Data & Analytics Lead:** owns technical quality and maintainability.
- **End users:** participate in UAT.

## Delivery Roles
| Role | Expected contribution |
|---|---|
| Business Analyst | Define conduct questions, escalation use cases and acceptance criteria. |
| Data Governance Analyst | Govern product definitions, firm identifiers, context measures and data quality. |
| Data Engineer | Prepare repeatable ingestion and analytical model from FCA workbook. |
| Conduct/Data Analyst | Analyse complaint rates, upheld patterns, closure performance and outliers. |
| BI Developer | Create governed conduct-risk dashboard and drill-down. |
| Risk/Assurance Analyst | Challenge thresholds, interpretation and escalation logic. |

The intended organisational outcome is a trusted, repeatable decision process rather than a one-off report.

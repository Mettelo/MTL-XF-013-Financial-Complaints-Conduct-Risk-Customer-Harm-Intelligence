# Project Brief

## Client Commission
Senior management receives complaint totals but cannot reliably identify which products, firms or complaint patterns may indicate deteriorating customer outcomes. Volumes, upheld rates, closure performance and contextual denominators are reviewed separately.

The team has been commissioned to answer:

> **Where should conduct and customer-outcome teams investigate first, and what evidence should trigger escalation, remediation or enhanced monitoring?**

## Primary Dataset
**FCA Firm-Specific Complaints Data — 2025 H2**  
Publisher: Financial Conduct Authority  
Official source: https://www.fca.org.uk/data/complaints-data/firm-level

## Required Delivery Lifecycle
**Discovery → Requirements → Governance → Acquisition → Engineering → Data Quality → Analysis → Decision Framework → BI/Product → Assurance/UAT → Executive Recommendation → Handover**

## Cross-Functional Roles
| Role | Expected contribution |
|---|---|
| Business Analyst | Define conduct questions, escalation use cases and acceptance criteria. |
| Data Governance Analyst | Govern product definitions, firm identifiers, context measures and data quality. |
| Data Engineer | Prepare repeatable ingestion and analytical model from FCA workbook. |
| Conduct/Data Analyst | Analyse complaint rates, upheld patterns, closure performance and outliers. |
| BI Developer | Create governed conduct-risk dashboard and drill-down. |
| Risk/Assurance Analyst | Challenge thresholds, interpretation and escalation logic. |

## Core Business Questions
1. What is the current baseline and where is material variation concentrated?
2. Which definitions and data-quality issues could change the decision?
3. What segments, organisations, products, areas or cohorts require deeper investigation?
4. Which indicators are appropriate for prioritisation and which are not?
5. What intervention options are supported by evidence?
6. How should management monitor the decision after handover?

## Success Criteria
- traceable requirements and acceptance criteria;
- governed definitions, ownership, lineage and quality rules;
- reproducible ingestion/transformation;
- defensible complaint volumes in context, upheld rates, closure timeliness, product patterns, firm variation and emerging conduct indicators;
- transparent prioritisation logic;
- useful decision-support product;
- UAT/assurance evidence;
- executive recommendation and handover;
- visible team contribution.

## Review Panel
Mettelo reviewers may challenge requirements, definitions, quality, analytical assumptions, thresholds, model use, governance, usability, recommendations and maintainability.

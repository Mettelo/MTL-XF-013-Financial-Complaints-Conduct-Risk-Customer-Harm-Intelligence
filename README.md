# MTL-XF-013 — Financial Complaints, Conduct Risk & Customer Harm Intelligence

**Mettelo Project Studio — Cross-Functional Delivery Series**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Client | Confidential Regulated Financial Services Group |
| Sector | Financial Services / Conduct / Customer Outcomes |
| Duration | 8 weeks |
| Recommended team | 6–8 |
| Sponsor | Director of Conduct Risk |
| Engagement | Simulated confidential-client engagement |

Senior management receives complaint totals but cannot reliably identify which products, firms or complaint patterns may indicate deteriorating customer outcomes. Volumes, upheld rates, closure performance and contextual denominators are reviewed separately.

**Commissioned decision:** Where should conduct and customer-outcome teams investigate first, and what evidence should trigger escalation, remediation or enhanced monitoring?

This is an end-to-end multidisciplinary engagement, not a dashboard-only exercise.

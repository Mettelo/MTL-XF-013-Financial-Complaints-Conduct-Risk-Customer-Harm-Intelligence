Senior management receives complaint totals, but volume, upheld rate, closure timeliness and contextual denominators are reviewed separately. This makes it difficult to distinguish scale effects from genuine deterioration in customer outcomes or conduct risk.

**Decision to support:** Which firms, products or complaint patterns should be escalated for conduct investigation, remediation review or enhanced monitoring, and what evidence is strong enough to justify that escalation?

This is an end-to-end delivery engagement, not a dashboard-only exercise.
